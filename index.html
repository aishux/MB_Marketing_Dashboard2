Definitions and sources

Single data source: the Daily MW Report - Non Cohort report in Power BI, with the report's own filters, validated cell-by-cell against the 8 Sep 2026 exports: Business_Source = Marketing, sources containing "expo" excluded, BDT sales desk excluded, demo-competition campaigns excluded, Remarketing category excluded, countries by actual country. CPC = Spend/Clicks, CPL = Spend/Leads, CPA = Spend/Live Accounts, CPFA = Spend/Funded Accounts (funded in the period, any acquisition vintage), Avg Account Size = FTD $/Funded Accounts, Redeposit Total = the report's Re-Deposit measure. Expected comes from the Lead Distribution / Marketing Budget plan (13 Aug 2026): country budgets plus targets (PPC: CPC $7, CPL $116.67, CPA $333.33, CPFA $1,111.11 · Social: $1, $16.67, $47.62, $190.48). All Countries is the sum of the 23 country plans. Green: on plan or cost at/below target. Orange: 60–90% or cost up to 1.5x. Red: below 60% or cost above 1.5x. Yesterday and Last 7 days show actuals only. TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn + Snapchat and Programmatic currently have no active spend. Recent-day numbers keep updating as ad-platform and CRM data backfills.
